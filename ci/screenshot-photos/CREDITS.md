# Screenshot demo photos

Real photos used **only** by screenshot mode (`SCREENSHOT_MODE=1`) to give the
seeded demo catches, crew posts and friend avatars real pictures. They are not
part of the app bundle: the screenshots workflow copies this folder into the
simulator app's `Documents/ScreenshotPhotos/` after installing the build, and
`ScreenshotSupport` reads them from there.

Every image is public domain or CC0 (no attribution legally required, no
watermarks), checked on the source page. Most are US government works (US Fish
& Wildlife Service, US Forest Service, National Park Service). They were found
through [Openverse](https://openverse.org) and downloaded from the original host.
Files are cropped and resized from the originals.

Note: several photos show identifiable people. Public-domain/CC0 status covers
copyright, not publicity rights; the people are not endorsing Currents, and the
names shown in the app are fictional.
