Currents is a fishing companion that works where the fish are: on the water, often with no signal. Your forecast, maps and logbook live on your phone, so nothing waits on a connection.

Know when they bite
Today gives you a bite score for where you are, the best windows of the day and the week ahead. It brings together pressure, wind, moon, solunar periods and tides, and shows each one so you can see why the day looks good or slow. Pick a target species and the forecast tunes itself to that fish.

Know what to throw
Lure and colour suggestions for the conditions and water clarity right now, drawn from the gear you own and what has worked in your own catches.

Explore the water
A map of dams, lakes, rivers and coast with a bite score on every water. See what is biting near you, save your spots, find boat ramps and download areas for offline use before a trip.

Keep every catch
Log a catch in seconds with photos, length, weight, gear, depth and water clarity. Currents identifies the fish from a photo on the device, records the conditions at the time, and strips location data from photos before anything is shared. Track sessions with a route on the map, keep your personal bests, build your Fishdex and keep a fishing streak going.

Fish with your crew
Start a crew with your fishing friends. Share catches, react and comment, plan group trips and run tournaments with live standings and a podium. Earn achievements and wear the title you unlocked.

On your wrist and Home Screen
Check the bite and log a catch from Apple Watch. Widgets show today's bite score and give you one-tap logging, and Live Activities keep a session or tournament clock on your Lock Screen.

Also included
Size and bag limits where they are published, a licence wallet with expiry reminders, a knot library, gear tracking with barcode scanning, Siri shortcuts, optional iCloud sync between your devices and full export of your data.

Your catches and spots stay private unless you choose to share them.
