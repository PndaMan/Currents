Currents is a fishing app that works where the signal doesn't. The forecast, the map, the catch log, fish ID and the field guide all run on the phone. No account, no subscription, and nothing leaves your device unless you join the Community and choose to share it.

THE BITE, HOUR BY HOUR
A 0 to 100 bite score for right now, and a timeline you can scrub through the day. Each hour shows its score, the change from now, and why: pressure trend, solunar majors and minors, tide, golden hours, moon, wind, and whether the water temperature suits what you're after. Best Windows pulls out the top feeding periods of the day. Seven days of forecast, and a day-by-day outlook in the planner for trips up to two weeks out.

What to Throw reads water clarity, temperature, light, wind and pressure and ranks lures and baits to match. Anything already in your tackle box floats to the top.

THE MAP
Tap any patch of water and Currents tells you whether it's worth a cast: the weather right there, the full score breakdown, likely spots ranked by what's been caught nearby, and your own waypoints. Save the tap as a spot in one press. Layers for public boat ramps and access points (with directions), nautical depth, rain radar, animated wind and current, and satellite. Regions download for offline use.

THE LOG
Photo first. Species, weight, length, water temp, gear, notes and the bite score at the moment of capture. Drop the pin anywhere, not just where you're standing. Point the camera at the fish and measure it in AR, and if you didn't bring a scale the app estimates weight from length. Swipe to favourite, long-press to delete. First of a species or a new personal best gets confetti. Streaks, personal bests per species, a best-hours heatmap, and which gear actually produces.

FISH ID AND THE FIELD GUIDE
Take the photo and the on-device classifier suggests the species. The photo stays on your phone. The Field Guide has 1,500 plus species drawn in the app's own style, and catching one fills it in. Seasons shows what's biting this month, by habitat and water temperature.

SESSIONS
Start a GPS session from the phone, the watch or Siri. A Live Activity keeps the clock, distance and catch count on the Lock Screen. When you stop, Currents finds the places you lingered and offers to save them as spots, then renders a recap card with the route, the stats and the day's fish for sharing. The planner holds a date, a forecast pin and a gear checklist to tick off.

GEAR, KNOTS, LICENCES
Photograph your tackle, build loadouts, scan a barcode to tag line and hooks for reordering. An illustrated knot library, from the improved clinch to the dropper loop and the snell. A licence wallet reads the expiry off a photo or PDF and reminds you before it lapses. Size and bag limits for South African waters, with the obvious caveat that you confirm the local rules yourself.

COMMUNITY (OPT-IN)
Off until you turn it on. Friends by six-character code, and leaderboards for most fish, heaviest and longest. Share a spot with a friend as an exact pin or a rough area. Crews have a shared feed with reactions and comments, a weekly challenge sized to the crew, and captain, mate, admin and member roles. Group trips put everyone's catches on one feed with live standings. Tournaments score 10 a fish, 1 a kilo and 5 for the first of a species, or set your own rates, with live standings and a Lock Screen card showing your team against the team to beat. It all syncs through your own iCloud, so there's no server and no password.

WATCH, WIDGETS, SIRI
Say "three pound bass released" to your watch and it logs the species, the weight and the release. Bite score and fish-on complications. A quick-log widget and a next-session widget. Ask Siri for the bite score, or to start and end a session.

Over 130 achievements in six tiers up to mythic, and you can wear an unlocked title under your name. Everything exports to CSV whenever you like. Weather from Open-Meteo. Requires iOS 26.
