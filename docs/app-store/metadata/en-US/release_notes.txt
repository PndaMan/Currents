Comments, challenges, and a lot more badges.

• Crew posts take comments now. Reply, edit (it shows "(Edited)"), and moderators can delete.
• One weekly challenge per crew, picked from what the crew actually catches and sized to the roster, with a countdown in the crew page.
• Tournaments: set your own points per fish, per kilo and per first-of-species. Share a tournament card. The Lock Screen shows your team against the team to beat, with a countdown when there's an end time.
• The leaderboard has its own screen. Most Fish is a podium, Heaviest and Longest are photo boards, and you can flip between friends and each crew.
• Achievements go to 130-odd badges in six tiers. There's a mythic tier now, and one hidden badge. Unlocked titles can be worn under your name.
• Confetti for a first-of-species or a new personal best, from the phone, the watch or Siri.
• "Three and a half pound bass released" now logs species, weight, length and release status by voice.
• Boat ramps and public access points on the map, with directions.
• Profiles show catches as a photo gallery.
• A new welcome flow, haptics on filters, and a confirm step before deleting catches, gear or presets or ending a group trip.
