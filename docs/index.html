Currents

Fish smarter. Log everything.

A fishing app for iPhone that works where the fish are, often with no signal. Free, with no ads, no subscription and no account.

What it does

  • A bite score for where you are, built from pressure, wind, moon, solunar times and tides, with each factor explained.
  • Lure and colour suggestions for the conditions, from the gear you own.
  • A map of dams, lakes, rivers and coast, with offline areas you download before a trip.
  • A catch log with photos and fish identification that runs on the phone.
  • Crews, group trips and tournaments with live standings, if you want them.
  • Apple Watch logging, widgets, licence reminders, knots and size and bag limits where they are published.

Your data stays yours

Catches and spots live on your phone unless you choose to share them. Currents needs iOS 26 or later. The code is open source on GitHub.
