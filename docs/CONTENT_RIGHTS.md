# Content rights & provenance

A record of where every piece of user-visible content in Currents comes from,
so App Store submission's "you must have rights to all content" attestation can
be answered confidently, and so the marketing screenshots stay rights-clean.

## Fish artwork (`fish_<id>` asset catalog)
- **Origin:** Generated by this repo's own pipeline (`.github/workflows/fish-art.yml`,
  `scripts/generate_fish_art.py`) using Google Gemini image generation.
- **Rights:** Original works produced for this app; no third-party photographs
  are bundled. Used freely in-app and in marketing screenshots.

## Knot & rig diagrams (`Resources/Knots/*.jpeg`)
- **Origin:** Freshwater and Marine Image Bank (FMIB) vintage engravings.
- **Rights:** Public domain. Attributed in-app ("Illustration: Freshwater and
  Marine Image Bank (public domain)").

## Maps
- **Base map:** Apple MapKit. Permitted in-app and in screenshots of your own app.
- **Optional overlays (OFF by default):** OpenSeaMap seamarks (OpenStreetMap /
  ODbL), RainViewer radar, satellite tiles. These carry their own licenses and
  are attributed in-app when enabled. They are **left off in screenshot mode**,
  so no third-party map imagery appears in App Store screenshots.

## Species facts & taxonomy
- **Origin:** FishBase / iNaturalist (names, families, temperature ranges,
  conservation status). Facts and taxonomic data are not copyrightable.
- **iNaturalist photos:** NOT bundled. Fetched at runtime for the species-detail
  view only, shown with attribution. Screenshot mode never navigates to that
  screen, so no externally-licensed photo is ever captured.

## App Store screenshots
See `.github/workflows/screenshots.yml`. Captured in the iOS Simulator (no
physical device needed) at iPhone 16 Pro Max / 6.9" (1320×2868). "Screenshot
mode" (`SCREENSHOT_MODE=1`) seeds demo catches whose photos are rendered from
the app's **own** fish artwork — every pixel shown is owned or public domain.
